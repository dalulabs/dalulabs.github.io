Privacy Policy for Alibi

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Alibi (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is a logic-puzzle game published by Dalu. This policy explains what data the app handles. The short version: we don't collect any.

1. Information we collect

None. Alibi has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking tools. We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers, or any other personal information.

2. Data stored on your device

The following is saved only on your device, so you can pick up where you left off: your puzzle progress (which files you've opened or solved, your marks, mistakes, hints used and the time spent on each file), the days on which you solved a daily file (used to show your streak), your theme, sound, haptics and timer settings, whether you have finished the training file, and whether you have bought full access. On the website version it is kept in your browser's local storage. This data never leaves your device, and we cannot see it.

Deleting the app, or clearing your browser's site data, removes it permanently. A purchase is not lost this way: you can get it back with “Restore purchase”.

3. Purchases and updates

Alibi offers one optional in-app purchase, “Full Access”. It is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details, your Apple Account or your name; the app only learns from Apple whether the purchase has been made. Apple's own privacy policy applies to the payment.

To deliver new files and fixes without a full app update, the app asks Expo's update service (expo.dev) whether a newer version of the game content is available. Like any request on the internet, this includes your IP address and basic technical details such as the app version and platform, which Expo uses only to deliver the update. We do not receive or store this information.

4. Sharing

We do not sell or share any data with third parties. The app has nothing to share: it sends nothing about you or your play to us or to anyone else.

5. Children

Alibi contains no violent or mature content and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

6. Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app adds features that handle data, such as optional usage statistics or advertising, we will update this policy before that version is released and change the date above.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy or the app: dalulabs.app@gmail.com
