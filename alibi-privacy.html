Privacy Policy for Alibi

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Alibi (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is a logic-puzzle game published by Dalu. This policy explains what data the app handles. The short version: we collect nothing ourselves. If you choose to watch an ad, Google's ad service receives some technical data to show it.

1. Information we collect

We collect none. Alibi has no accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics. We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers, or any other personal information, and we never see any data about you.

2. Data stored on your device

The following is saved only on your device, so you can pick up where you left off: your puzzle progress (which files you've opened or solved, your marks, mistakes, hints used and the time spent on each file), the days on which you solved a daily file (used to show your streak), your theme, sound, haptics and timer settings, whether you have finished the training file, and whether you have bought full access. On the website version it is kept in your browser's local storage. This data never leaves your device, and we cannot see it.

Deleting the app, or clearing your browser's site data, removes it permanently. A purchase is not lost this way: you can get it back with “Restore purchase”.

3. Purchases and updates

Alibi offers one optional in-app purchase, “Full Access”. It is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details, your Apple Account or your name; the app only learns from Apple whether the purchase has been made. Apple's own privacy policy applies to the payment.

To deliver new files and fixes without a full app update, the app asks Expo's update service (expo.dev) whether a newer version of the game content is available. Like any request on the internet, this includes your IP address and basic technical details such as the app version and platform, which Expo uses only to deliver the update. We do not receive or store this information.

4. Optional ads

Players without Full Access can choose to watch a short ad to open one locked file. Ads are never shown on their own, never interrupt a file, and are never shown to anyone who has bought Full Access. They are provided by Google AdMob. When you open the purchase screen or watch an ad, Google's ad software on your device may send Google your IP address (which gives an approximate location), basic device and app information (including an identifier that is shared only between apps from the same developer), and information about the ad, such as whether it was shown and watched. Google uses this to show the ad, to measure it and to prevent fraud.

Alibi asks Google for non-personalized ads only and never asks for permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites, so it does not use your device's advertising identifier. Where the law requires it, for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, you are asked for consent before any ad is requested. Google's own privacy policy applies: policies.google.com/privacy, and how Google uses data from partner apps.

5. Sharing

We do not sell or share any data with third parties. Apart from the ad requests described above, which go directly from your device to Google, the app sends nothing about you or your play to us or to anyone else.

6. Children

Alibi contains no violent or mature content, and we collect no personal information from anyone, including children. Alibi is not directed at children under 13.

7. Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes how data is handled, we will update this policy before that version is released and change the date above.
